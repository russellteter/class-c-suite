Methodology & Built-In Assumptions
Full transparency on how this model computes
Cash baseline: The Finance team's weekly cash forecast (May 10 snapshot) is aggregated to month-ending balances. May 2026 = $1.99M (W22 ending 5/31). Trough = $112K (W30 ending 7/26). Year-end Jan 3 2027 = $2.89M. This baseline contains all bonus payments, legal settlements, board fees, AWS ramp-down, Coso TSA receipts, BME collections, and other items as forecasted by Finance.

Tranche layering: Each tranche's monthly cash impact is computed as (gross savings) - (severance) and applied as a delta to the Finance Baseline:
  • T1: Selected employees × their fully-loaded comp ÷ 12 = monthly savings, starting June 2026 (per direction). Severance paid in May 2026 = sum of (fully_loaded_cost × months_per_location / 12).
  • T2: Selected Czech employees' monthly comp added as savings from October 2026 (5-month notice during which they remain on payroll — this period is already in the Finance Baseline). Optional lump severance paid in September 2026.
  • T3: Per-employee savings from September 2026. Severance paid in August 2026.
  • T4: Per-employee savings from January 2027. Severance paid in December 2026.
What's NOT modeled (deliberately): Operational damage / revenue impact (separated to qualitative panel). M&A scenarios. Refinance scenarios. Coso-TD Bank release. Barclays BACA release. FX changes. Tax payments. Inflation. Salary raises.
