## Framing: What Power does Class actually have?

Before picking a steelman, force the Helmer question — because every steelman is implicitly betting on a different Power thesis.

| Power | Class evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Switching Costs** | $100K+ Class accounts = 100% GDR; LTI-based Zoom/Teams integration deployed inside customer LMS workflows; audit-ready records for compliance. **REAL but only in large enterprise** | **PARTIAL** — only at $100K+ tier |
| **Counter-Positioning** | Zoom/Teams "engagement layer" thesis. But Zoom + Microsoft can build this themselves and Teams Premium is already encroaching. **WEAK** | **WEAK** |
| **Scale Economies** | $20M ARR, no infrastructure leverage vs Zoom/Cisco. **NONE** | **NONE** |
| **Process Power** | Renewal team that has learned how to land Collab→Class migrations + multi-currency overrides. Possibly real but lives in heads, not codified. **THIN** | **THIN** |
| **Branding** | NPS −16 (Class −11, Collab −28). Negative brand equity. **NEGATIVE** | **NEGATIVE** |
| **Cornered Resource** | International retention 87% vs NA 72.6% — Massimo/Emmanuel/Niko/Petya hold relationships that Class as a corporate entity does not. **YES but in people not company** | **YES — in individuals** |
| **Network Effects** | None. **NONE** | **NONE** |

**The integrated Pass 2 + Step 2 position is implicitly betting on Process Power emerging from the new pricing model + tiered ICP framework + Russell taking the revenue org.** That's a bet that Class can build Process Power it does not currently have, while losing the Cornered Resource (Massimo expensive to keep, expensive to fire) and continuing to bleed Branding (NPS unaddressed).

**This is a thin Power thesis.** That's what creates room for a real steelman.

## 1. LEAD STEELMAN — "Renewal-only mode + run for cash, position for M&A by Q4"

**(A hybrid of candidate paths #2 and #4. The combination is stronger than either alone — the M&A exit becomes credible precisely because the operating story is renewal-only discipline, not another year of "we're pivoting to a new ICP.")**

### The Core Thesis

Class is not a growth company. It has been a renewal-dominated business for at least 18 months — 89.9% of LTM closed-won is Renewals, 4.9% is genuinely new business, and net-new-logos (Account.Type = Prospect) is **four accounts / $98K** over the trailing year. The integrated position's bet — that a tiered ICP redesign + new pricing + Russell taking the revenue org will materially shift this — is asking the system to do something it has never done in the period for which data exists.

**The steelman path:**

1. **Stop pretending to be a growth company.** Cut Dalton + the outbound motion entirely. Cut the Healthcare ABM Round 2 spend. Cut the Training Provider campaign rollout. Cut Kendall's role to brand-maintenance only. Approximate savings: $250-400K/yr in burn + Manila BPO costs once that audit completes (separate workstream Q6).
2. **Concentrate every dollar on renewal defense + pricing discipline on the existing book.** Sabina's team is the company. Robert Thayer's $3.24M book, Emmanuel's $2.27M EMEA book, Niko's $2.19M LAC book, Andee's $2.41M book — this is where the dollars live. Add a dedicated renewal-engineering function (one analyst) to industrialize the 70% Collab→Class migration workarounds into a standard playbook.
3. **Aggressively push the new May 1 pricing model onto the underwater accounts on a renewal-date schedule** (Q11 + Q12 deliverable becomes the central operating workstream, not a queued follow-on). 66 underwater accounts at $1.27M/yr leakage + Tier 9-11 HED margin compression is the largest controllable dollar lever in the business — bigger than any new-business outcome being targeted for FY26.
4. **Engage M&A advisor in June, run process Q3-Q4.** The story to a strategic acquirer (Anthology, Instructure, D2L, Cornerstone, Docebo, 360Learning) is: "$20M ARR, 87% International GDR, codified pricing model, clean renewal book, layer-on-top-of-Zoom/Teams that you'd take 18 months and $15M to build internally." That's a defensible 2-3x ARR conversation in a market where strategic SaaS multiples in this segment are weak but acqui-hire-plus-book deals trade.
5. **Russell takes the COO/President role in service of the runoff + sale**, not a turnaround. Decision rights in writing tied to renewal performance + sale-readiness, not new-business targets.

### Why this is BETTER than the integrated position

**(a) The integrated position is asking Class to acquire a Power it does not currently have.** Process Power in a tiered-ICP motion takes 18-24 months of disciplined execution with a real CMO, a real marketing-ops stack, attribution that works, and a CPO with an AI vision. Russell has none of those today. WS-08 is admittedly dark ("no vision for what AI feature would actually matter"). The Healthcare proof asset (Tampa General) is "being shy for some reason" — meaning the named customer hasn't agreed. Kendall is "Creative Director, Brand and Customer Marketing" — not a demand-gen leader. The integrated position requires building 5+ capabilities in parallel inside a cash-constrained, ARR-cliff-visible company. **It is a 24-month plan being asked to deliver inside a 12-month cash runway.**

**(b) The Cornered Resource (the international AMs) is fragile, not durable.** Massimo at $409K loaded + 26-week Italian severance is the textbook expensive-to-keep / expensive-to-fire trap. If he leaves voluntarily — which is exactly what happens when senior people read the financials and the COO change — International GDR collapses from 87% toward the NA 72.6% line, and the trailing-book renewal cliff accelerates 12-18 months. The integrated position has no defense against this scenario. The renewal-only steelman addresses it head-on: lock the Cornered Resources with retention bonuses tied to sale completion, not new-business quotas.

**(c) Q3 FY26 is the cash event that matters, and it is INSIDE the integrated position's execution window.** $9.83M renewing in Q3 at last year's Q3 GDR (75.7%) = $2.39M probable churn hitting cash W36-W44. That is *before* any new ICP motion has had time to produce closed-won. The integrated position will be 12 weeks into a 24-month plan when the cash event hits. The renewal-only steelman puts every resource on the Q3 renewal cohort with 12 weeks to work.

**(d) The "new business covers 23% of expected churn" math is decisive.** The integrated position improves this ratio at the margin — maybe from 23% to 35% over 18 months in an optimistic scenario, never to 100%. A business where new business cannot cover churn is, by definition, in runoff. Runoff is a strategy. Pretending it isn't, is not.

**(e) The Hamilton Helmer read is unambiguous.** Class has Switching Costs in the $100K+ enterprise tier and Cornered Resource in 4-5 international AMs. **It does not have Branding, Scale Economies, Network Effects, or Counter-Positioning.** It has speculative Process Power that requires capabilities Class does not yet possess. A defensible business strategy concentrates on the Powers you have and monetizes them. The renewal-only steelman concentrates on Switching Costs (defend the $100K+ tier ruthlessly) and Cornered Resource (lock the AMs), and then converts those Powers into a sale at the moment of maximum defensibility — which is right now, before the Cornered Resource erodes and while the pricing model is freshly consolidated.

**(f) It is what the financials are already telling Holdco.** Holdco recapped through the preferred stack zeroed out — they have already taken the loss on the equity story. Their remaining return scenarios are (i) sale, (ii) eventual recap, (iii) wind-down. **All three are served by the renewal-only steelman; only (i) is served by the integrated position, and it requires the integrated position to succeed first.** The renewal-only steelman is the dominant strategy under Holdco's actual incentive structure.

### Conditions that make this correct

- Q3 FY26 GDR comes in below 80% (continuing the FY25 trend)
- One or more of Massimo / Emmanuel / Niko shows departure signals in next 60 days
- Healthcare proof asset (Tampa General) does NOT land by Aug 1 (signal that customer-side trust gap is structural)
- Barclays signals impatience with ARR trajectory in any Q2/Q3 review
- Any inbound from a strategic — Anthology already in the partner ecosystem and on the cap table indirectly via Holdco, Instructure/D2L are obvious — would tip this to certainty

### Who would prefer this

- **Chasen.** It's the path that produces an exit event he can run, with a story he can tell. He gets out from under the operating problem.
- **Holdco.** Maximizes recovery on the recapitalized stack. Removes the operating risk. No additional capital required.
- **Barclays.** Cleanest credit story. Renewal-only mode is the most predictable cash flow profile possible — they don't care about ARR growth, they care about debt service coverage. Renewal book at $17.55M with 80% GDR + new pricing = the most defensible coverage story available.
- **Russell himself.** His MIP cap is $675K and only triggers on a sale. His Class E units are worth $0 without a sale. He has a Good Reason termination that is narrow and a Cause clause that is broad. **The only path that materially monetizes his equity is a sale.** The integrated position is a 24-month operating slog that produces no equity event. The renewal-only-into-sale steelman is the path that aligns his own incentives — and aligns them with Holdco's — most cleanly.
- **The Cornered-Resource AMs.** A sale with retention bonuses pays them out cleanly. A 24-month turnaround grind with declining headcount and no equity event does not.

### Who would oppose this

- **Ed Miller** — but he's exiting in 30-45 days, so he doesn't get a vote on the next chapter.
- **Kendall + Dalton + the BPO contractors** — but they have no decision authority and their roles are economically marginal.
- **Russell's own narrative preference** — he's said "hate having to constantly pivot and chase the next hunch of sales." But this isn't a pivot — it's the opposite of a pivot. It's accepting what the business is.

## 2. SECONDARY STEELMAN — "Concentrate hard on HED, accept the trailing book" (candidate #3)

**Briefly.** HED is 47.9% of trailing ARR with 444 customers we already know how to serve. International HED retention is 87% vs NA 72.6%. The forward HED pipeline being $6.5K / 29 opps at $224 avg isn't a sign HED is dead — it's a sign Class stopped feeding HED leads to the funnel and put all marketing dollars elsewhere. **Russell's Step 2 Q1 answer confirms this: "Zero marketing dollars or demand-gen investment" on HED.** That is a strategic choice the company made, not a market verdict.

The steelman path: Reverse the choice. Reinvest a Kendall-equivalent + 2 SDRs against International HED specifically (EU, ANZ, LATAM HED — the strongest retention cohort in the company). Run RMIT, UNSW Sydney, Edinburgh, Sheffield, Curtin as flagship references — these are Tier 1 universities, which is the strongest possible reference logo set in HED. Kill the Healthcare ABM (no proof asset). Kill the Training Provider campaign (no scale evidence yet). Accept smaller deal sizes ($25-50K vs $216K Corp Training Co) in exchange for higher win rates, faster sales cycles, and renewal stickiness in the segment Class actually wins.

**Why it's second, not first:** It's a strong segment-strategy steelman, but it doesn't address the runoff math. Even if HED retention goes from 72.6% NA to 85% blended through reinvestment, new business cannot cover churn in a $20M ARR business with declining win rate (22% → 11%) and declining deal size ($35K → $21K). It buys time and improves the renewal book — both real wins — but it doesn't change the strategic destination. Whereas the lead steelman accepts the destination (sale) and concentrates resources accordingly.

The two steelmans are NOT mutually exclusive. **You could run the secondary steelman as the operating thesis FOR the lead steelman** — i.e., reinvest in International HED retention specifically as the renewal-defense + sale-prep motion, while quietly running the M&A process. This is probably the strongest synthesized position of all.
