## 1. Position

**Class is not a GTM company today — it is a renewal-harvesting company with a forward-pipeline thesis bolted on.** 89.9% of LTM closed-won is Renewals ($17.55M); New Business is $963K across 66 opps with only 4 true net-new logos at the Account.Type level (CB §1, §6). Three quota carriers, one named SDR, no CMO, marketing reporting to the CRO, and a single individual (Massimo Gentili) producing $2.55M LTM — roughly 2.6x the entire global new-business engine (CB §3). The story I tell the board and Barclays is the truthful one: **Class sells an engagement layer on top of Zoom/Teams to VILT-heavy buyers — and the existing book is HED-skewed (47.9% concentration per POS-004) while the forward motion has decisively pivoted to Corporate Training Cos, Healthcare VILT, and Training Providers** (CB §2: Corp Training Co $863K open, Healthcare VILT $846K open, vs ICP HED $6.5K / 29 opps / $224 average). Why customers buy: real-time engagement visibility, audit-ready attendance/competency, breakout management at scale — anchored in two active campaigns (Training Providers economic-defensibility and Healthcare EHR-training ABM on 933 accounts, CB §5). The CEO read of revenue defensibility is bifurcated: **the $100K+ Class cohort runs 100% GDR (zero churn); the <$10K cohort runs 41.1% GDR** (CB §6). The strategy I will execute is **diversify the forward book away from HED while protecting the $100K+ enterprise cohort like a hostage** — because that cohort is the only thing that survives a sale-process diligence room. Helmer would call this a thin "Process Power" position (the engagement layer is undifferentiated alone) propped up by emerging "Counter-Positioning" (Zoom/Teams-overlay vs Engageli's full-stack rebuild). Christensen lens: we are not the disruptor; we are the incumbent's friend, which is sustainable but unsexy. Watkins 100-day: I am past day 1; the GTM diagnosis is done, the prescription is segment + pricing + AI repositioning, and the clock is the July 26 cash trough plus the FY26 Q3 renewal cliff ($9.83M / 49% concentration in the same quarter that ran 75.7% GDR last year — CB §6).

## 2. Top 3 risks from the CEO lens

**Risk 1 — The board narrative depends on a forward pivot that has zero published proof.** I am telling the board that Healthcare VILT and Corp L&D are the future, but my own marketing team has no customer quotes for the new segment (CB §1: Russell asked, silence followed, 😞). Round 2 of the Healthcare ABM is running on 933 accounts (CB §2, §5) and I have a 2.2x pipeline coverage on Class vs the 3-4x industry rule (CB §1). If Q4 misses, the narrative shifts from "pivoting" to "in denial." Board-narrative impact: severe. Barclays-narrative impact: this is the line item they will challenge in any covenant reset conversation — "show me the new-logo proof."

**Risk 2 — The renewal engine is the entire business and it is structurally weakening.** Combined GDR 79.1%, NRR 76.9% (CB §6) — both well below SaaS median. Q3 FY26 holds $9.83M of renewals (49% concentration) in the same quarter that posted 75.7% GDR last year, with $4-5M already flagged "at risk" across Zero Usage / Severe Decline / Possible Drop buckets (CB §6). A 5pp GDR miss on Q3 alone = ~$500K ARR walking out the door in the cash-tightest stretch of the year. For Barclays, this is the covenant story: ARR walking out faster than new logos can replace it makes any future EBITDA forecast a fiction. POS-004 is half-right — concentration is the trailing-book risk; the forward risk is renewal velocity in NA (72.6% GDR vs International 87.0%, a 14.4pp gap that is the actual retention crisis, CB §6).

**Risk 3 — Pricing operations are broken at the exact moment we need pricing leverage.** Order Form Generator caps at 50% discount, Collab→Class migrations require 70% workarounds (Niko/Sabina, CB §1, §4), at least 5 active pricing docs in last 30 days with no canonical source (CB §7), and Russell on record that "uniform/standard discounting policies" are still pending. Grove SIP: this is the inflection point where the legacy product's economics are inverting — Collab is being migrated INTO Class and we are losing pricing integrity in the transition. For Barclays, broken pricing ops = unmodelable ASP = the diligence question I cannot answer cleanly in a sale-process data room.

## 4. Quantitative anchor

**$4-5M of FY26 renewal ARR is flagged at-risk across Zero Usage, Severe Decline, and Possible Drop buckets — out of $20.32M FY26 renewal book = 19.7-24.6% of the renewable book is on a watch list** (CB §6). ## 5. Decision-rights question — the single CEO decision in the next 60 days

**Do I formally retire ICP HED as a forward-investment segment, or do I keep it as a "harvest and protect" tier while pivoting marketing/SDR dollars 80% to Corp Training Cos + Healthcare VILT?**

Three options:

- **Option A — Full retirement of HED forward investment.** Communicate to the board and Barclays that HED is a $14.7M ARR harvest book, no new SDR, no new marketing, AE bandwidth reallocated. Cleanest narrative; highest-velocity diversification. Risk: if Q3 HED renewals miss harder because we stopped feeding the segment, the cash trough deepens.

- **Option B — Tiered focus.** Keep HED for $100K+ enterprise accounts only (where GDR is 100%), retire HED <$10K (where GDR is 41.1%). Reallocate SDR + ABM to Corp/Healthcare. Most defensible to Barclays — "we are protecting the durable cohort and shedding the unprofitable one." Risk: still a HED-concentration story in the data room for 12+ months.

- **Option C — Status quo + wait for FY27 budget cycle.** Defer the call until after Q3 renewal cycle closes. Risk: WS-09 stays YELLOW, the board narrative weakens with every quarter of HED contraction (CB: −6.4% renewal contraction on tagged HED), and the diversification proof point I need for any sale-process conversation never arrives.

**My lean is Option B.** It is the only one Barclays will read as discipline rather than panic, and it preserves the $100K+ cohort that is the only piece of this company a strategic acquirer actually wants. Decision required by 2026-06-30, communicated to the board at the July 8 meeting, and operationalized in WS-09 motion v1 by 2026-07-01.
