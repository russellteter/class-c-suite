## 1. Position — the CMO read

Class today is positioned as **"the engagement layer on top of Zoom and Microsoft Teams"** — real-time engagement visibility, role-based breakouts, audit-ready attendance/competency tracking. That positioning is coherent on paper and has two live validation experiments running right now: the **Training Providers campaign** (class.com/training-providers/, persona email copy, business-case one-pager, Kendall-led, economic-defensibility narrative) and **Healthcare ICP Round 2** on 933 accounts with the **ICP EHR named-account campaign on 30+ Tier A+ targets** (EHR training as "highest-stakes virtual training a health system runs"). Both campaigns are pitching a positioning the brand has not yet earned: **there is zero published customer proof in healthcare** (Russell asked his team for healthcare quotes, got silence, reacted 😞), `MainCompetitors__c` has **zero records across 1,579 LTM closed opps**, and the internal AI-native repositioning conversation **does not surface in 90 days of Slack search** — meaning if a Healthcare CLO or a Training-Provider CEO asks "who else uses you in our segment?" or "what's your AI story?", the field has no answer to hand them. The brand is at risk of becoming **the Zoom/Teams add-on that pivoted segments faster than it built proof in any of them** — a generalist engagement-layer SKU sold mostly on renewal inertia ($17.55M of $19.53M LTM = 90% renewal), with new-logo motion small enough ($963K LTM, only 4 true Account.Type = Prospect wins) that the market can't actually see us in the new ICPs we're claiming. Madhavan Ramanujam's pricing-as-positioning frame matters here: when the Order Form Generator caps at 50% discount and Collab→Class migrations require **70% workarounds**, the price the market actually transacts at is invalidating the premium positioning the campaigns are selling. Pricing IS the positioning, and ours is leaking.

## 2. Top 3 risks (CMO lens)

**Risk A — The customer-proof void in net-new ICPs (CRITICAL).** Healthcare VILT has 2 renewals and $93K LTM ($141K avg open pipeline deal); Training Provider has the webpage and the assets but no anchor logo story. We are running a three-tier ABM campaign on 933 Healthcare accounts and a 30-account Tier A+ EHR push with **no testimonial, no case study, no quote, no logo wall** for either segment. Sales gets one shot at a Tier A+ CLO and walks in without a peer reference. This is the single most-fixable brand failure: identify the 3 best healthcare references in the existing book (even small/legacy), get on the phone this week, ship one case study and three pull-quotes before Round 2 sends. If we don't, Round 2 will produce the same meeting-to-close gap as Round 1, and the **Healthcare $846K open pipeline (avg $141K) becomes the data point that "Healthcare doesn't work for Class"** — when the real failure was that we marketed to a segment we hadn't sourced proof in.

**Risk B — Marketing attribution is broken; the board-follow-up "marketing-sourced pipeline %" will be guessed, not measured (HIGH).** **62% of new-business won-$ has NULL LeadSource.** Of the $963K New Business LTM: SDR-sourced (Dalton) = 43% ($412K), NULL = 40% ($385K), Channel = 16% ($156K), AE = 1%. A defensible marketing-sourced pipeline number cannot be reconstructed from current data — best-case estimate is **8-15% marketing-sourced** (assuming a fraction of the NULL bucket traces to demand-gen rather than AE/relationship-sourced; healthcare and training-provider campaign meetings are explicitly handed to Daniel and would show as AE-sourced if logged at all). When Russell answers board follow-up #1 with a number, the number will be directionally honest but methodologically indefensible. Without LeadSource discipline plus campaign-influence multi-touch in HubSpot, **we cannot prove the $400K-$446K of Marketing Programs spend produces pipeline** — and in a cash crisis, unprovable spend gets cut.

**Risk C — Competitive intel is orphaned; positioning is being authored without competitor input (HIGH).** `MainCompetitors__c` = zero records. The 20 "Lost to Competitor" deals ($1.36M, 10% of lost-$, avg $68K — competitors only beat us on the larger deals) are uncoded. Engageli and Top Hat are monitored externally per the adversarial index, but **zero Slack mentions of competitor names in 90 days** means the field is not surfacing what they hear in deals. The Training Providers campaign claims "economic defensibility" without naming what we're defending against (Docebo? Intrepid? in-house?). The Healthcare EHR brief positions against "tools that weren't built for it" without naming Zoom Webinars, Teams Live Events, or the EHR vendors' native LMS. **You cannot do positioning without a competitor.** Currently we are.

## 3. What the CMO needs (one ask each)

- **CEO:** Sign a 60-day **brand discipline mandate** — no segment pivots, no new ICP framings, no new "AI-native" public language until Healthcare proof + Training Provider proof are shipped. The market can absorb one pivot per year, not three. Lock the Zoom/Teams engagement-layer narrative as the canonical 2026 story; the AI-native repositioning is WS-08 product work, not a marketing campaign.
- **CFO:** Of the $400K-$446K Marketing Programs line inside the $2.8M S&M budget, **re-allocate ~$75K-$100K from net-new lead-gen to proof-asset production** (case studies, video testimonials, healthcare/training-provider analyst briefings, third-party validation). Net-new ad spend without proof assets is lighting money on fire. Also: fund HubSpot at the 300K-contact tier to preserve attribution continuity (Russell already flagged this).
- **CRO (Ed Miller):** Commit the AE team to **populating `MainCompetitors__c` and `Lost Reason detail` on every closed-lost opp ≥$25K starting tomorrow.** No SDR meeting accepted into a closed-lost without a competitor code. This is the single change that lets marketing produce a battlecard worth using by July 1.
- **Chief of Staff:** Own the **monthly "Marketing Truth" review** — one page, four numbers (marketing-sourced pipeline %, healthcare funnel conversion, training-provider funnel conversion, NPS combined), reviewed with Russell + Ed + Kendall. Without an enforced cadence, this data dies in HubSpot.

## 5. Decision-rights question (next 60 days, marketing-only)

**Does Class commit Q3 FY26 marketing dollars + Kendall's + Roxana's + Dalton's bandwidth to a single ICP (Healthcare OR Training Providers), or split across both?** Ed Miller as marketing org-head can ratify, but the **only function that can author this decision is marketing** — it requires a brand/positioning judgment (where does the story land hardest with proof we can ship in 60 days?), not a quota judgment. Healthcare has higher strategic resonance with the board narrative but a deeper proof void. **Recommendation: concentrate Q3 on Training Providers, ship one healthcare proof asset by Aug 1, defer healthcare-scale spend to Q4 once proof exists.** This is the single decision that, made in the next 14 days, changes what the brand looks like in 90 days. Made in 60 days, it's too late for Q3.
