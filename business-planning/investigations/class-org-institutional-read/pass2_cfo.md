## Top 3 risks (CFO lens)
1. **Severance timing means cuts don't help July.** POS-006 codifies CFO spread-mode severance is legally required. Cuts decided in June-July land their savings in Q4 at earliest, with a one-time lump in the July trough window. Net effect: any "save the company via headcount" thesis is mathematically blocked from saving July 26. Cuts only matter for Q4+ runway, which means they must serve the team-I-need question, not the trough question.
2. **The Czech engineering bench at $47-131K FL each is the highest-leverage cost center to PROTECT not cut.** Engineering production capacity per FL$ is concentrated here. Cutting any of Michal, Bronek, Ivo, Pavel, Robin, Jakub, Marek, Jiri, or Abhinav destroys 5-10x their cost in product velocity. The CZ statutory notice is also 5 months — so cuts would land their savings even later, with worse net cash effect.
3. **The off-roster contractor population (Manila BPO + Vivek + Sales Ops Mgr + CoSo extras) is not in the lever model.** If those are paid through AP rather than payroll, they are deferrable in a cash crunch but Russell needs to know the AP exposure. If they are 1099s or BPO-billed, they can be terminated at very short notice — which is both a defense lever AND a fragility risk (terminate one Manila contractor, the funnel dries that day).
