## Steelman: the strongest alternate read

**Alternate position:** Russell should NOT consolidate Marketing + Renewals + CS + Sales under COO. Instead, Russell should explicitly partition the company into Operations (his domain) and Revenue (Chasen's interim, then an external hire) — and BRING IN an external CRO/Chief Customer Officer in Q3 to take everything GTM-facing off Russell's plate.

**Why a smart counterpart would prefer this:**
1. Russell's stated walk-away leverage doctrine (POS-005) only works if Russell is *not* irreplaceable. By consolidating GTM + Marketing + CS + Operations under him, Russell becomes the operating linchpin — which paradoxically *destroys* his walk-away leverage. The COO who can't leave is not a COO with leverage.
2. The company in cash crisis cannot afford for Russell to also be doing Marketing's job, Renewals' job, CS's job, AND the entire operations job. The bandwidth math doesn't work — Russell has 12 workstreams (WS-01 through WS-12) PLUS Locality on the side PLUS the apply campaign as walk-away. Adding "operate the GTM org day-to-day" is over-allocation.
3. An external CRO/CCO hire has a market — there ARE turnaround-stage CROs who would take a 12-month contract for upside-only comp at a SaaS company with $20-35M ARR. Hiring one signals the company is operating with adult supervision; not hiring one signals Russell is doing everything because no one else is competent — which is a board-readable signal too.
4. The redesign that retains Russell as "Operating + Strategic + COO + interim CRO + interim CMO + interim CCO" is a redesign that has no exit — there's no off-ramp for Russell because there's no one to hand any of it to.

**Conditions under which the steelman beats the working position:**
- Russell's bandwidth in next 60 days collapses below ~50hr/week sustainable (board prep + cash defense + multiple 1:1s + redesign + Locality + apply campaign = arithmetic suggests this is the actual scenario).
- An external CRO/CCO can be sourced in <60 days for a turnaround comp package the company can afford (probably equity-heavy, modest cash).
- The redesign creates a CRO seat that's career-attractive enough to recruit into.
