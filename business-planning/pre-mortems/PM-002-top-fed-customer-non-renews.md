## Scenario
Largest federal contract enters renewal window. Contracting officer signals re-compete. ARR impact ~$3.2M. Competitive RFI activity in the procurement portal. Class is the incumbent but the win is no longer assured.

## Early-warning signals
- Contract POC turnover at the agency
- Late-stage RFI from competitors
- Delayed renewal admin (signature requests sitting >2 weeks)
- New procurement or finance contact appearing in late-cycle calls (per call-intelligence pattern)
- Drop in QBR attendance from agency leadership

## Mitigation (preventive)
- Executive sponsorship cadence — Russell or Chasen direct touch quarterly
- Deliver an unexpected upgrade in Q2 (commitment from product roadmap)
- Pre-engage the contracting officer's leadership 6 months before re-compete

## Response playbook
- Shift to lower-tier contract vehicle (preserve some ARR even if main contract lost)
- Offer staged extension (90-day bridge while procurement finishes)
- Engage incumbent-protection arguments before re-compete is formalized
- Notify Barclays before they see ARR drop in reporting (PM-001 prevention)
