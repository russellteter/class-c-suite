## Scenario
Coordinated procurement freeze across EU university systems compresses Int'l HED segment 30% in single budget cycle. ARR impact ~$5.2M. Precedent: 2024 freeze affected 3 of Class's top 10. With 47.9% concentration today, this is the single largest plausible structural risk.

## Early-warning signals
- NRR on segment drops below 95% for two consecutive quarters
- >2 named accounts signal budget delay
- Sector-press coverage of EU higher-ed austerity
- Drop in segment-wide renewal pipeline activity in Salesforce
- Late-cycle procurement contacts replacing original sponsors (call-intelligence pattern)

## Mitigation (preventive)
- Diversification investment in K-12 and corporate L&D verticals starting now (WS-09)
- Reduce segment concentration target from 47.9% to <35% over 12 months
- Pre-emptive pricing flexibility envelope for at-risk segment accounts
- Executive sponsorship cadence across top-10 segment accounts

## Response playbook
- Emergency segment-rebalancing plan
- Pricing flexibility for at-risk accounts (defined envelope, fast approval path)
- Communications to Barclays BEFORE they see ARR drop in reporting (PM-001 prevention)
- Activate WS-12 M&A optionality if segment collapse is irreversible
