Recommended Actions — Prioritized

The math says: pursue the levers with biggest cash impact AND lowest operational damage. In order:

1
OPEN BARCLAYS REFINANCE CONVERSATION THIS WEEK. Converting cash interest to PIK saves $2.88M/year — bigger than ALL four tranches combined, with zero operational damage. This is the single highest-leverage decision in the model.
2
EXECUTE TRANCHE 1 (S&M cut + AWS savings). Best $-saved-per-risk-point ratio of any cut. Modest, surgical, defensible. $135K/month from June plus the AWS savings already booked.
3
DO NOT execute Tranches 2, 3, or 4. Combined, they add only $50K of ending cash vs Tranche 1 alone — while removing 12 more heads, costing $700K+ in severance, and eliminating the company's product/G&A/sales capability. This is what "already cut too deep" looks like in the math.
4
CONFIRM Coso-TD Bank ($3.245M) release schedule. Could push trough comfortably above $1M without any other action. Single Knox SPA review.
5
DOCUMENT Barclays BACA ($2.5M) access mechanism with counsel. Pre-run the covenant trigger drill so you're not making decisions in a panic if the trough is breached.
6
INTENSIFY M&A timeline. If a buyer is real, deal close by Q3 2026 is materially better than any operational outcome. Critically: executing Tranches 2-4 destroys buyer optionality — the cuts make the business less attractive to acquire.
7
IF M&A is not real or slips past Q4 2026: model a soft wind-down. The wind-down scenario delivers similar cash ($7.1M) to "all tranches" ($7.4M) but with zero operational damage and full optionality preserved.
8
STOP framing the tranches as "the plan." They are tactical levers with diminishing returns and escalating operational damage. The actual strategic plan must include refinance, M&A, or restructuring — and the model can support whichever path you choose.
