## Recommended ask range for the COO role

**Anchor logic (without Irina's number):**
- Floor: CFO-equivalent OTE = **$325K OTE** ($225K base + $100K bonus). You should not be moved into the COO chair at the same OTE you have today. That's not a promotion.
- Mid: $275K base + $125K bonus = **$400K OTE**. Aligns with the CJ Johnson "Chief" loaded-cost peak and gives Chasen a number he's seen at the Chief-level table before.
- Stretch: $300K base + $150K bonus = **$450K OTE**. Likely at or above Irina's package and pushes against board cash-crisis optics, but is defensible against external COO benchmarks for a $30M+ ARR SaaS company.

**Non-cash levers that should be in the ask regardless of base/OTE:**
- **Severance: extend from 4 months → 9–12 months** (with CIC / change-of-control double-trigger acceleration). In a company with an explicit 12-month survival ceiling, this is the single highest-EV term you can negotiate. Cash cost to the company today is zero; downside protection for you is enormous.
- **Equity refresh / retention grant** — most companies layering on a COO step-up add a meaningful new option grant with accelerated vesting on involuntary termination or sale.
- **Bonus floor / guaranteed minimum first-year bonus** — given that hitting variable targets in a cash-crisis turnaround is uncertain, ask for a guaranteed portion (e.g., 50% paid regardless of attainment in Year 1).
- **Title clarity:** "Chief Operating Officer" vs. "COO and President" or "COO of Class and Coso" — the broader the title, the harder it is to be down-leveled later.
- **Board exposure:** observer rights or formal presenter slot on board calls. Hardens your external market position.

- The company is in cash crisis → they cannot afford to lose another senior person on top of Tomas + Sharae exits
- The COO role is the operational glue holding the survival plan together → without you, the plan has no operator
- BUT: cash is the binding constraint → asking for big base increases will be resisted on optics. Asking for severance, retention equity, and bonus floors costs them nothing today.
