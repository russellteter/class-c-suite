**Severance — 7-day lump payment**

- Your move: "Lump within 7 days is critical to me because of where the company is. If Class is in distress when I'm terminated, 12 months of installments isn't going to get paid. Lump within 7 days isn't more money — it's just timing."

- Walk-away: must get at least first 3 months as a lump within 30 days

## How to wield the leverage without being overt

The leverage is real but should never be spoken. Here's what to do and what to avoid:

**DO:**
- Open the conversation with: "Michael, I've spent serious time thinking about whether I can step into this role given where we are. I want to. But for me to do this well, here's what I need."
- Be specific and prepared. Show up with the numbers written down.
- When pushed back, pause. Don't immediately concede. Say "Let me think about how to make that work" and come back next session if needed.
- Frame the protective asks as "this is what allows me to commit fully — without these, I'm going to be distracted by the wrong things."
- Reference market: "An external COO offer at this level would carry [X]. I'm not asking you to match external — I'm asking you to make it a real promotion."

**DO NOT:**
- Say "or I'll leave." Don't even hint at it directly.
- Apologize for the size of the ask.
- Cave on the first counter. The first counter is never the final counter.
- Discuss other offers you may or may not have. Keep the external option vague and implied, not concrete.
- Negotiate component-by-component in real time on a phone call. Take time between rounds.

**The hardest moment will be:** when Chasen says "we just can't do that — you know where we are." Your move is to acknowledge the constraint and redirect: "I do know where we are. That's exactly why I'm asking for these specific things — I need to be able to focus on saving the company without worrying about whether I'll be financially exposed if it doesn't work."

|---|---|

| Severance months | <5 months |
| Severance shields | No bankruptcy filing trigger |

| Title | "Acting COO" or "Interim COO" |
| **Total Y1 floor** | **<$340K (which is ~$10K above external alternative — not enough premium for the additional risk)** |

If ALL of these are at threshold simultaneously, the package is structurally worse than walking. If ONE is at threshold but others are above, you're still likely better off taking it.

**The single best summary heuristic:** if Y1 guaranteed cash floor (base + variable floor + sign-on) is below **$350K AND severance shields don't include at least the bankruptcy trigger and 7-day lump on first 3 months**, walk.
