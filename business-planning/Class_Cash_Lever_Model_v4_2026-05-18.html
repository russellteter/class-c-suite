📐 Severance Calculation Legend (Per CFO Policy)

Severance Amount = Base Salary × Weeks ÷ 52
Severance Range = 2 to 12 weeks depending on tenure and seniority. Defaults below are estimates; replace with actual employment agreement terms.
Cash Timing — Spread mode (default per CFO policy where legally allowed):
  • Person stays on payroll at normal rate for weeks weeks after termination decision
  • During this period: no change vs baseline — they're still being paid like normal
  • Savings begin AFTER spread period ends
  • Example: terminate end of May with 8 weeks spread → spread runs through Aug 2 → savings begin Aug 3. July sees zero cash benefit.
Cash Timing — Lump mode:
  • Severance paid in full at termination as one outflow
  • Savings begin immediately the next week
  • Example: terminate end of May with 8 weeks lump → -$X cash hit May 31 → savings $Y/wk begin June 7. July gets full month of savings.
Czech employees: The 5-month notice period is already in the Finance Baseline payroll line (they continue receiving normal salary during notice). The Weeks input applies to ADDITIONAL lump severance AFTER notice ends. Notice begins at termination decision; savings begin 5 months later regardless of mode.
Ongoing savings basis = Fully Loaded annual cost ÷ 52 per week (because Finance Baseline payroll includes the fully-loaded amount).
