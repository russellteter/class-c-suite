## Claim
The CFO's insistence on spreading severance across normal payroll (vs lump-sum) is a legal posture against constructive-dismissal and acceleration-of-deferred-compensation arguments, not a finance preference. Reversing this requires counsel sign-off, not CFO sign-off.

## Evidence
- CFO severance policy memo (2025-11) — explicitly cites employment counsel guidance
- Pattern across all 2024-2026 exits from Class — spread mode used in every case
- Czech 5-month statutory notice — additional legal binding for EU staff
- Memory `cfo_severance_policy.md` — codified

## Dependencies
- None

## Counter-evidence (would force revision)
- Counsel approves a lump-sum exception in writing for a specific cohort
- Change in jurisdiction or applicable employment law

## Operating implication
Headcount-cost-savings modeling must use spread-mode timing always. This is what makes POS-002 (July trough is headcount-irrelevant) load-bearing — the legal constraint, not a discretionary CFO choice, is why cuts can't help July.

Any conversation about accelerating severance economics must start with counsel, not CFO. Russell should not push CFO to deviate; that's the wrong door.
