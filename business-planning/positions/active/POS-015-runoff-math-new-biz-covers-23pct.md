## Claim
At Class's current GTM coverage ratio, new business + expansion cannot replace renewal churn. The math is determinative: $17.55M LTM renewal book × (1 − 79.1% GDR) = $3.67M expected gross churn. New-biz LTM closed-won is $963K. Expansion LTM is $1.0M. Combined growth motion = $2.0M offsetting $3.67M churn = **shortfall of $1.7M ARR per year, before any segment-specific risk**. The shortfall widens in the compound-default scenario (POS-013 Q3 cliff at 65% retention floor → $3.4M Q3 alone). Class is, mathematically, a managed-runoff business. The integrated GTM motion (Pass 2 position) is the right operating posture for runoff but it is not a growth thesis.

## Evidence
- SF Closed Won LTM by Type 2026-05-21: Renewal 644 opps / $17.55M / avg $27.3K; Expansion 32 / $1.00M / avg $31.4K; New Business 66 / $963K / avg $14.6K; Product Migration 16 / $10K
- class-gtm-data key-numbers.md FY25: GDR Combined 79.1%, NRR Combined 76.9%
- Russell answer Q1: "Thinly resourced and its very hard to be nimble with such little available budget"
- Marketing budget FY26 Proj: $446K total / $400K Marketing Programs — small
- Sales coverage: 3 quota carriers with $512.5K total Q2 quota
- Math: $17.55M × 0.209 churn rate = $3.67M expected churn; growth motion $1.96M; coverage = 53% (BEFORE accounting for compound default); new-biz alone covers $963K / $3.67M = 26% of expected churn; including expansion = 53%
- Note: 23% figure used in CEO/CFO lens conversations is new-biz-only coverage; including expansion = 53% — still well below 100% replacement

## Dependencies
- FY26 GDR does not catastrophically deteriorate below 70% (compound default)
- The 4 forward ICPs do not generate a 3-4x step-change in new-biz $ within 6 months
- Massive expansion within the existing base does not appear (no signal it will)

## Counter-evidence (would force revision)
- Healthcare ICP closes 5+ deals at $150K+ ACV by Aug 31 — would change new-biz trajectory
- Training Provider promotion campaign produces 10+ new logos with referral expansion attached
- Pricing model anchoring drives renewal-up-sell ARR change to +5% segment-wide (vs current +0.83%)
- M&A optionality materializes — the math becomes irrelevant
- Major retention turnaround — Q3 FY26 GDR lands at 87%+ (matching International benchmark)

## Operating implication
The integrated GTM motion's tactical actions are NOT designed to reverse the runoff math — they are designed to:
1. Slow the runoff rate (better retention via anchoring + targeted renewal motion)
2. Defend the largest accounts (Cornered Resource + Switching Costs)
3. Generate enough new-biz to demonstrate operating discipline + defensible motion for an acquirer

The strategic frame (from POS-014 + steelman synthesis) is M&A by Q4 or Q1 FY27. The COO offer must reflect Russell taking the operating function of a runoff company moving toward sale, NOT a growth company moving toward expansion. Specifically:
- WS-09 GTM Refinement target metrics should be retention-up + new-biz-as-acquirer-proof, NOT new-biz growth targets that the math says are infeasible
- Marketing program $ should weight toward proof-asset production (anchor of Pass 2 CMO lens recommendation) + ICP campaign execution, NOT brand-build for expansion
- WS-10 COO comp negotiation reflects this scope — Russell is taking on a managed-runoff + M&A-positioning operating role, not a growth-CRO role
