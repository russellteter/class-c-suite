## Context
Ed Miller (CRO) is expected to exit within ~30 days per Russell's read 2026-05-21. Russell has been de facto CMO for ~9 months; Marketing already reports to him in practice. The COO transition (WS-10 in motion) absorbs Ed's remaining scope: Daniel Hansen (Sales Corp/Gov), Massimo Gentili (VP International), Sabina Cramer (SVP Renewals), and the CS layer via Sabina/Jorge.

## Options considered

**Option 1 — Consolidate under Russell.** Russell absorbs CRO scope fully when Ed exits. Marketing formalizes under Russell publicly. Sabina's reporting line moves to Russell (or she exits — see DEC-007). External CRO is NOT hired.
- Pros: clean lines; no recruiting cycle during cash crisis; brand/retention sponsored at C-table level
- Cons: Russell's bandwidth concentration is real; POS-005 (silent leverage) weakens; if Russell exits, no GTM continuity

**Option 2 — External CRO/CCO hire on turnaround comp.** Russell stays COO; bring in external GTM leader on equity-heavy comp to absorb sales+renewals+CS while Russell focuses on operations.
- Pros: preserves Russell's bandwidth; external lens on the GTM motion; restores POS-005 leverage
- Cons: 60-90 day search; turnaround talent is limited; would dilute Russell's authority during a sensitive transition

**Option 3 — Promote internally to CRO.** Promote Massimo, Sabina, or external-but-quick to fill Ed's seat.
- Pros: continuity; less cost than Option 2; faster than Option 1's full absorption
- Cons: no internal candidate is positioned (Andee = strong IC not manager; Sabina = under evaluation; Massimo = INTL-focus, possible de-resource)
- Why rejected: no viable internal candidate per the institutional read

## Decision
Option 1 — Russell absorbs CRO scope, with explicit bandwidth tripwires that activate Option 2.

## Rationale
Marketing absorption is already factual (~9 months de facto). Sales + Renewals + CS absorption is the incremental step. POS-007 codifies the bandwidth thesis at confidence 65. The steelman (Option 2) remains the right path IF bandwidth fails — explicit tripwires below.

## Assumptions that must hold
- Russell sustains <60hr/week through end of Q3 (PRED-001)
- Daniel Hansen does not voluntarily exit in next 90 days (PM-008 at 25%)
- Sabina retention strategy resolves cleanly (DEC-007 in progress)
- Chasen ratifies the absorption (WS-10 closure)

## What's NOT a blocker on this decision
Public announcement sequencing of Ed's exit vs Russell's COO move. Per Russell 2026-05-22: "This 'announcement' is something we can figure out whenever. It's not hard. We're a tiny company." Comms logistics are not a decision gating mechanism. Sequencing can be resolved when Russell + Chasen have a 5-minute conversation any time before either announcement lands.

## Tripwires that force reversal to Option 2
- Russell reports >65hr/week sustained for 3+ weeks consecutively
- Two or more of {Daniel exit, Sabina cascade, Robert/Holly manage-out, Massimo INTL-de-resource exit} fire simultaneously
- Board signals discomfort with Russell-as-sole-GTM-owner
