## Options considered

**Option A — Retain Sabina; she reports to Russell.** Direct reporting line absorbs the renewal team management.
- Pros: continuity on $13M+ renewal book; no recruiting cycle; no 12-wk severance
- Cons: Russell carries her behavioral cost; "expensive and challenging to work with" pattern continues; she may exit anyway in solidarity with Ed

**Option B — Elevate Sabina with title change (Chief Customer Officer or equivalent).** Promotion-not-demotion framing; reports to Russell but with elevated title.
- Pros: comms reads as a promotion; possibly retains her through the transition
- Cons: doubles down on the cost; no real change in operating dynamics

**Option C — Plan for Sabina's exit; promote internally.** Andee ruled out (Russell: "Strong IC, not yet manager material"). External hire or contractor-bridge needed.
- Pros: aligns with Russell's read; ends the difficult relationship
- Cons: 12-wk EU statutory severance; succession gap during the transition

**Option D — External hire on turnaround comp.** Bring in a Head of Renewals/CS externally to replace Sabina.
- Pros: fresh leadership; no continuity from the older axis
- Cons: 45-60 day search; team disruption; cost

## Rationale
Sabina decision is not gated by a 1:1 with her. It rests on assembling the informed picture from sources the system already has + can pull: her SF activity history (the renewal book she actually runs vs. what's delegated), Chorus call signal on her management style and customer relationships, the CFO severance model on 12-wk EU statutory cost, her compensation benchmark vs. SVP-Renewals market data via ZoomInfo, and the operational read on whether her team performs better with her or with a different leader (Andee ruled out as successor, but external/contractor-bridge possible). The 1:1 — if Russell chooses to have it — is one calibration input, not the gating mechanism.
